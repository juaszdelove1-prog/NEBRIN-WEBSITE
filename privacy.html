PRIVACY POLICY

How NEBRIN handles customer information

NEBRIN collects only the information needed to process service requests, appointments, payments and customer support.

Information collected

Names, phone numbers, email addresses, selected services, uploaded documents, payment proof and appointment details.

Use of information

Information is used to process requests, communicate progress, verify payment and deliver completed services.

Document security

Customer documents are stored in private storage and are accessible only to authorized administrators through temporary links.

Retention and deletion

Invalid applications may be marked deleted and their uploaded documents removed. Minimal audit information may be retained for accountability.
